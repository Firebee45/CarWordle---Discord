# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 August 2026

## What we collect

The bot may store your Discord user ID and server ID to make commands work. The activity page does not collect any personal data itself.

## What we don't collect

We don't collect messages, emails, passwords, or any data beyond what's needed for the bot to function.

## How data is used

Stored IDs are only used to run bot commands and are not sold or shared with third parties.

## How to delete your data

Message the bot owner on Discord and any stored data linked to you will be removed.

## Changes

This policy may be updated at any time. Continued use after changes means you accept the new policy.

## Contact

Questions can be sent to the bot owner directly on Discord.
